MAZE

You are trapped inside a multi-staged maze, your goal is to find the escape ladders and survive across all the stages and reach the end. And for a real challenge? Switch to Hard Mode to awaken the Ghost of an older player who failed to escape... now hunting the ones who try to escape—a relentless entity roaming the maze that hunts you down.

Desktop Controls: • WASD or Arrow Keys: Move
• Mouse: Look around (Click screen to lock cursor)
• Spacebar: Jump
• Shift: Sprint
• Esc: Pause Menu
Mobile / Touch Controls: • Left Joystick: Move
• Right Screen Swipe: Look around
• Green Button: Jump
• Top-Right Icon: Pause Menu
